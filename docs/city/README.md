# City mockup

These three pages are the approved City mockup. They are the source that City mirrors, and each City section copies its markup from them while the frame is built.

- `Main.dc.html` is the desktop City page
- `Phone.dc.html` is the phone layout
- `States.dc.html` shows the empty, loading and error states

The files are kept byte for byte as the owner approved them. Change the City build to match them, and change these pages only when the owner approves a new mockup.

`support.js` is the design tool's runtime. The pages load it from `./support.js`, but it is not copied into this repository, so the pages do not run here as they do in the design tool. Read them for their markup and styles.

Every City figure comes from what OliverCode records, such as the repository, the backlog and the run log. A figure is read from those records when City renders, and no figure is typed into City by hand.
